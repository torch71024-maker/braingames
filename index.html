Pattern Recognition · Cognitive Flexibility · Rhythm

ECHO
Auditory Discrimination · Tonal Memory
Tones play through your speakers or headphones. Press the button matching the pitch. Tones get closer together and shorter as levels rise.

REFLEX
Reaction Time · Impulse Control
Targets flash on screen — tap them fast. But when they turn red, stop. Trains both speed and the brakes. Gets faster, smaller, and harder to inhibit.
PLAY →
STROOP
Inhibitory Control · Cognitive Flexibility
A word appears in a conflicting color. Click the ink color, the word, or the odd-one-out color — the rule switches each level. Harder than it sounds.
PLAY →
SCAN
Visual Search · Peripheral Attention
A grid of similar symbols — one is different. Find it before time runs out. Grids get larger and differences grow subtler. Trains the visual system to cover the whole field.
PLAY →
RECALL
Working Memory · Sequential Processing
Buttons light up in sequence. Reproduce it exactly. Sequences grow longer, colors multiply, pace quickens. Trains the mental workspace used in everything.
PLAY →
TRACK
Visual Tracking · Sustained Attention
Dots light up then blend into a moving swarm. Track your target through the chaos — tap it when they freeze. Harder levels add more targets and more dots.
